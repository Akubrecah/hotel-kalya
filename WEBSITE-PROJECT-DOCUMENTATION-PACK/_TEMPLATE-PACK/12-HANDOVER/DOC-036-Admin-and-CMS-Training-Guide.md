## 1. Staff Login & Portal Access

1. Open `https://www.clientdomain.com/login`.
2. Enter staff credentials (`[staff@clientdomain.com]`).
3. The platform automatically detects staff permissions and routes to the **Administrative Portal (`/admin`)**.

---

## 2. Front-Desk Console Operations

1. **Reviewing Daily KPIs:** Check live Occupancy Rate, Kitchen Orders in Prep, Active Reservations, and Gross Revenue.
2. **Managing Guest Folios (`/admin/reservations`):**
   - Search guest by name, phone number, or booking reference.
   - Filter by status (`Confirmed`, `Checked-In`, `Completed`, `Cancelled`).
   - Click "Check-In" when guest arrives at front desk.
   - Click "Print Voucher" to generate a physical paper copy if needed.
3. **Managing Kitchen Orders (`/admin/orders`):**
   - New tickets appear in "New Orders".
   - Click "Start Prep" to move to "Cooking & In Prep".
   - Click "Mark Ready" to alert dining staff.
   - Click "Delivered" once served to room/table.
4. **Room Inventory Management (`/admin/rooms`):**
   - Toggle room availability (`Available`, `Occupied`, `Maintenance`).
   - Flag housekeeping state (`Clean` vs `Needs Cleaning`).
