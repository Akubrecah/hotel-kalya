## 2. RACI Matrix

- **R (Responsible):** The role that performs the activity.
- **A (Accountable):** The individual with final approval authority (only 1 per activity).
- **C (Consulted):** Subject matter expert providing vital inputs.
- **I (Informed):** Kept updated on progress and outcomes.
