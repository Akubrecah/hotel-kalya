## 1. Statutory Frameworks

1. **National Data Privacy Legislation:** (e.g., Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 / General Data Protection Regulation GDPR).
2. **Consumer Rights & Transparency:** Clear refund, cancellation, and transaction dispute disclosures.
3. **Electronic Transaction Regulations:** Consent protocols for SMS, email notifications, and mobile money payments.

---

## 2. Mandatory Web Disclosures

- **Privacy Policy (`/privacy`):** Explicitly identifies data controller, data collected, lawful basis for processing, retention periods, and guest data rights.
- **Terms of Service (`/terms`):** Check-in/check-out policies, cancellation penalties, user conduct, intellectual property protection, and limitation of liability.
- **Cookie Notice (`/cookies`):** Explains local storage usage for digital carts and authentication session cookies.
