1. **Custom Client Assets:** All client logos, pre-existing trademarks, proprietary photos, and copy provided by the Client remain the exclusive intellectual property of the Client.
2. **Deliverables & Source Code:** Upon **full and final payment** of all invoices, the Developer grants to the Client full, unrestricted ownership of the bespoke source code, database schemas, and graphic layouts created under this Agreement.
3. **Developer Background Technology:** The Developer retains all rights to any pre-existing code libraries, open-source dependencies, boilerplate engines, and reusable development tools utilized in the delivery.

## 5. Client Responsibilities & Delays

1. The Client shall supply all required content, brand assets, and feedback within agreed timeframes (`DOC-015`).

## 6. Post-Launch Warranty & Support

1. The Developer provides a **30-day Post-Launch Warranty** commencing upon production deployment.
2. The warranty covers the remediation of software bugs and errors that deviate from approved specifications at no additional charge.
3. New features, layout redesigns, or third-party platform alterations are excluded from warranty and require a separate Maintenance Agreement (`DOC-039`).
