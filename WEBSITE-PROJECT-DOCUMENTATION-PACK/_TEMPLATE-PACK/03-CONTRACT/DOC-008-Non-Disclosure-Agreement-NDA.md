## 1. Purpose & Confidential Information

The Parties wish to explore and execute a business relationship concerning website and software development. In connection with this opportunity, each Party may disclose to the other certain proprietary and non-public information, including:
- Customer financial records, pricing models, and commercial strategies.
- API keys, server credentials, database connection strings, and architecture diagrams.
- Proprietary customer data subject to national privacy legislation (e.g., Kenya Data Protection Act 2019 / GDPR).

---

## 2. Obligations of the Receiving Party

1. **Duty of Care:** The Receiving Party agrees to treat all Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own proprietary information (and never less than reasonable care).
2. **Restricted Access:** Access shall be limited strictly to employees, contractors, and technical specialists who need to know such information for project delivery.
3. **No Disclosure:** Neither Party will disclose, publish, or disseminate Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent.

---

## 3. Exclusions from Confidentiality

Confidential Information does not include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement.
- Was already known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure.
- Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to disclosed information.
- Is required to be disclosed by judicial order or regulatory statute.

---

## 4. Term & Duration

The confidentiality obligations under this Agreement shall survive the completion or termination of the project for a period of **two (2) years** from the Effective Date.
