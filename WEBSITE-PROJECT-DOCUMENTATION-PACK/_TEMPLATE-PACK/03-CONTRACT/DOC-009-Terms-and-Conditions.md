**Document ID:** `DOC-009`

---

## 1. Engagement Framework

1. **Standard Working Hours:** Monday to Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:30 PM EAT.
2. **Authorized Points of Contact:** Only instructions issued or approved by the designated Project Manager (`DOC-003`) shall be deemed binding.

---

## 2. Invoicing, Payments & Suspension of Services

1. Invoices are payable within **14 calendar days** of issuance.
2. If Milestone payments are overdue by more than 10 business days, the Developer reserves the right to suspend development and staging server access until arrears are cleared.
3. Ownership of code, design files, and domain configurations remains with the Developer until full settlement of the final milestone invoice.

---

## 3. Delays & Project Dormancy

1. If the Client fails to provide requested feedback, content, or approvals for more than **20 consecutive calendar days**, the project shall be classified as "Dormant."
2. Reactivation of a Dormant project requires a formal schedule review and a mandatory **10% Project Reactivation Fee**.

---

## 4. Limitation of Liability

1. The Developer shall not be liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from third-party hosting outages, payment gateway API downtime, domain registrar suspension, or unauthorized client tampering with production code.
2. The total cumulative liability of the Developer under any claim shall not exceed the total fees actually paid by the Client under the contract.

---

## 5. Third-Party Licenses & Subscriptions

The Client is solely responsible for direct ongoing commercial subscriptions for:
- Domain renewal fees (`.com`, `.co.ke`).
- Cloud hosting & database infrastructure (Vercel, AWS, Supabase, Neon).
- Third-party APIs (Google Maps Platform, Safaricom Daraja, Twilio/AfricasTalking SMS).
