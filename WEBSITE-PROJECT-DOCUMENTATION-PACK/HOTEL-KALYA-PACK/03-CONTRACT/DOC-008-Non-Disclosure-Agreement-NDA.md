## 1. Context & Protected Information

This Mutual NDA protects confidential proprietary data exchanged between **Antigravity Digital Systems Ltd** and **Hotel Kalya Limited**, specifically:
- Safaricom Daraja API credentials, consumer secrets, passkeys, and shortcodes.
- Guest identity records, mobile numbers, booking folios, and payment audit logs.
- Hotel Kalya internal financial performance, room occupancy rates, and corporate group discount structures.
- West Pokot County leadership booking schedules and private banquet attendee lists.

---

## 2. Non-Disclosure & Data Protection Compliance

1. Both parties commit to strict adherence to the **Kenya Data Protection Act 2019**.
2. Zero secrets or environment variables (`.env.local`) shall ever be committed to public or private version control repositories.
3. Production database snapshots or guest booking databases shall not be retained on local developer machines following project handover.
