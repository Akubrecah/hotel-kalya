## 1. Business Deep-Dive

1. **What is your core business value proposition?**
   > Hotel Kalya is the premier hospitality landmark in Kapenguria, West Pokot County. We offer luxury executive suites, modern conference facilities, tranquil manicured gardens, organic farm-to-table cuisine (Kienyeji chicken, Lake Victoria Tilapia, Kapenguria Goat Nyama Choma), and serviced Airbnb cottages. Our tagline is *"Hospitality Redefined."*

2. **What are your company’s 1-year and 3-year strategic goals?**
   - 1-Year: Establish 100% digital presence, automate front-desk room allocations, reduce phone ordering friction via digital menu, and achieve direct M-Pesa reservations.
   - 3-Year: Become the official venue partner for all regional Western/North Rift cross-border summits, county government conferences, and tourism retreats.

3. **What is the single most important action a first-time visitor must take on the site?**
   > Check room availability and reserve directly, or browse the food menu to place an instant room-delivery or dine-in order.
