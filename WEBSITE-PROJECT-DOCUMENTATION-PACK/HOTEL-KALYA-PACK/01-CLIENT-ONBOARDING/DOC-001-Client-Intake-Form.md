## 2. Business Objectives & Website Mission

1. **What is the primary commercial reason for building this website?**
   > Transition Hotel Kalya from offline flyer-based operations into West Pokot County's premier digital hospitality platform. Eliminate phone booking bottlenecks, streamline room and conference reservations, and digitize food ordering.

2. **What are the top 3 problems you want this website to solve?**
   - Problem 1: County government and NGO conference organizers constantly ask for facility photos, hall capacities, and delegate packages.
   - Problem 2: Guests dining in rooms or arriving late have no structured way to browse the menu and order without waiting for staff.
   - Problem 3: Travelers on the A1 highway struggle with pinpoint turn-by-turn navigation and directions to the hotel.

3. **Who is your primary target audience?**
   - Primary: County government dignitaries, NGO workshop delegates, business travelers, transit motorists on the Kitale-Turkana corridor.
   - Secondary: Weekend families, couples booking garden photoshoot weddings, and extended-stay Airbnb guests.

4. **Who are your main regional competitors?**
   - Competitor 1: Mount Elgon Hotel (Kitale)
   - Competitor 2: Crane Suites / Local Kapenguria Guesthouses
