## 1. Compliance Implementation in Hotel Kalya

1. **Kenya Data Protection Act 2019:**
   - Dedicated route at `/privacy` (`src/app/privacy/page.tsx`).
   - Details handling of guest national ID/Passport numbers, mobile numbers for M-Pesa transactions, and room reservation data.
   - Designates `hotelkalya@gmail.com` as the Data Protection Liaison.
2. **Hospitality Booking & Garden Rules:**
   - Dedicated route at `/terms` (`src/app/terms/page.tsx`).
   - Formalizes 11:00 AM check-out, 12:00 PM check-in, deposit retention policies, and photoshoot permit rules for Kalya Gardens.
3. **Cookie & Web Storage Notice:**
   - Dedicated route at `/cookies` (`src/app/cookies/page.tsx`).
   - Discloses zero tracking or third-party behavioral advertising cookies, detailing necessary local storage keys (`hk_cart_items`, `hotel_kalya_auth_user`).
